# 21 · usb-drop

**What:** An awareness skit — no payload. When armed it throws up a red warning + alarm and prints
"the payload would already have run." The social-engineering half of BadUSB.

**Arm:** slide switch to the left. Toggle off to reset.

**Lesson:** USB-drop attacks work because a *person* plugs in a device they found. Studies show a
large share of dropped drives get plugged in. The human is the vulnerability.

**Defense:** policy + training ("don't plug in found devices"), USB port control, and disabling
autorun/HID on locked-down machines.
